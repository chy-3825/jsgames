Source: https://metool.online/zh/games/aeroplaneChess/

The project owner reported permission for the source material on 2026-08-17.

`board-sharp.png` is a cleaned, high-resolution derivative of the second
user-supplied sharpened PNG. It was edited with the built-in image generation
tool to restore the correct blue/green/red/yellow corner order, remove margins,
watermark and paper noise, and preserve the 52-space board geometry.

The four `*-plane.svg` files are the current resolution-independent plane
tokens. They preserve the circular token and top-down aircraft design with
antialiased vector edges. Superseded bitmap tokens and the low-resolution board
have been removed from the current-version directory.

The multiplayer state mapping, layout integration, controls, and animations in
this project are implemented locally in public/games/aeroplane/.
