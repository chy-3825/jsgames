## 四张续作的公共提示词

Create ONE Avalon evil-faction identity card, portrait 4:5. Supplied image is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: match its elegant matte gouache storybook brushwork, fine antique gold thorn border, courtly sophistication, restrained menace. Do NOT copy its woman, curtain or pose. Waist-up close character composition filling 85% frame, face and hands clearly readable, exactly two arms. Expensive well-kept clothing, dignified bearing and distinctive silhouette. Background is simple subordinate atmospheric setting specified below. Evil expressed through calculated expression, strong dark color masses, asymmetric shadow and thorn ornament, never neon, glowing eyes, skulls, gore or demons. No lettering, text, watermark, mockup.

## mordred

Mordred: aristocratic middle-aged royal pretender, neatly groomed short dark hair and short beard, angular proud face. Small restrained dark-gold princely circlet, superb engraved black ceremonial armor with deep oxblood royal mantle and heraldic clasp. UNIQUE ACTION: upright shoulders nearly frontal, chin lowered looking directly at viewer; one gauntleted hand adjusts the opposite glove cuff at waist, a controlled preparation rather than action pose. Background monumental empty throne hall, narrow pale light beam misses his face but strikes a vacant throne; black, oxblood and bronze palette. Elegant threatening royal authority, immaculate rather than rugged mercenary.
